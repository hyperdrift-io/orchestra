All capabilities and what the trial includes

Day one

First impression

It reads your app like a new customer.

Your Growth Manager reads your public pages and says what works, what’s in the way and the move to make first. Each finding links to the pages it came from, so you can check it. It also names your market and the audience your pages point to.

In the trial
A review of your website, the day you sign up.

Customers

It finds people your app could help.

Ask your Growth Manager for prospects or for investors. It reads their own pages and comes back with up to five, each with the public evidence that they may fit. A public page suggests a fit; only a reply confirms interest.

In the trial
Your first search: up to five prospects or investors.

Email

It writes to each, in your voice.

A first email for each, signed with your name, opening on a line from their own page. You read each one and decide. Approve them and they go to the CRM on your board with their drafts. Nothing leaves without your click.

In the trial
Each draft opens in your own mail app, or copies in one click. You send it yourself.
With the subscription
Connect your mailbox and each email leaves from your address, on your click. Replies land on that person’s record.

After day one

Plan

Ask in a sentence. Get a plan.

Tell your Growth Manager what you want, with a date. It comes back with a plan to your date and waits for your yes. Tell it what happened, and it works out what comes next from what’s on your board.

Market

It keeps up with your market.

Intel reads about fifteen sources for the market your pages named and brings you what moved, with the outlets that carry it. Your Growth Manager then proposes up to three moves, each answering one story. Take one and it becomes an initiative on your board.

In the trial
Up to three market reads, one a day, when we have coverage.
With the subscription
A fresh read each day you ask for one.

Results

Analytics matches your numbers to your initiatives.

Visitors, trials and paying customers on one line, with each initiative marked on it, so you can judge what each one returned and put your effort where it pays.

With the subscription
Analytics on your own app: the results of your growth, set against each initiative. Running today on Traction’s own board.

The Traction Partnership

How we work with you.

Traction gives your app a growth team. The Partnership adds ours: people who work in strategy, customer relationships and AI engineering, beside you on your board.

What we do together.

Choose the move.

One customer opportunity at a time, picked from the evidence on your board. We agree how to judge it and who does what before we start.

Do the work.

Research, outreach and customer conversations, done with you. Engineering when a move needs your product to change.

Measure it.

Every move and its result sit on the same board, so the outcome is yours to inspect.

AI prepares the research, the drafts and the recommendations. People agree the work with you, and you decide.

What we ask.

  • A working product, and a sign that people value it: paying customers, people coming back, repeat work.
  • You act within the week.
  • You tell us what doesn’t work.
  • You help another founder when it fits.

Why we’re building Traction

We believe there’s a short window for great ideas to make it. AI puts more within reach of a small team. We want to use that opportunity to get worthwhile ideas into people’s hands.

This is personal. Too many great ideas never find their market. We don’t want more founders looking back and wondering what could have been.

That’s why we’re building Traction. We’re bringing together a select few founders who do things today, not tomorrow. Keeping it small means we can work closely together and put our experience behind the ideas we believe in.

If you believe in what you’re building and you’re ready to give it your best shot, we can help. We’ll work with you to get it in front of the people it’s made for and act on what we learn.

You’ll help shape Traction too. Your feedback will guide what we improve as we work together.
