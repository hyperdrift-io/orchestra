# AI-native articles — concept brief

Status: three candidates for founder selection; no article-page implementation approved yet.

Mission: a reader sees a business capability they could use, can inspect the evidence, and knows how to start a conversation with Hyperdrift.

Approved editorial direction, 21 September 2026: the AI-native series belongs at ai.hyperdrift.io/articles, each article connects to an enquiry, and strong examples built by Hyperdrift support the argument. Existing Hyperdrift articles keep their URLs. The Bridge is the experience; the fleet is the collection of products; First Officer is the conversational role. The Crew remains a separate named service.

Visual scope: article opening only, not another homepage redesign. Preserve the AI site's approved golden-country world, the stone giant as a collaborator, warmth, human agency, and fine editorial typography. Exactly three comparable desktop images, 16:9, one per direction. Phone adaptation follows the selected concept; no desktop/mobile collage. No generated screenshots, charts, commercial results, metrics or testimonials. Actual evidence will be real media in the article body. All copy inherits meta/PHILOSOPHY.md §8, Speak to Enable.

Common copy: “The Bridge: start the day with decisions.” Secondary line: “The AI-native organisation · Hyperdrift”. The article reader can enquire about their own workflow; do not ask the image model to invent UI labels or form fields.

## A — The chart table

- Signature: a luminous chart on a warm stone table overlooking the golden country, a human hand indicating the next move and the giant's hand nearby.
- World: charcoal ink, parchment, warm stone, amber daylight; confident editorial type and generous calm space.
- Surfaces: reading feels like opening a considered field report at the place decisions are made; evidence figures sit like real exhibits.
- Motion: a quiet change of light on entry; static in reduced motion.
- Render: an exquisite article opening with the common copy and the chart-table signature, without a dashboard or invented diagram labels.
- Risk: too much atmosphere can hide the practical proof; keep the title unmistakable and the reading surface simple.

## B — The working journal

- Signature: an oversized open field journal, the golden landscape and the giant appearing in a single carefully composed illustration on its page.
- World: cream paper, dark serif ink, fine rules, ochre wash, tactile editorial print.
- Surfaces: the article is an inspectable account; the page gives text and actual evidence equal seriousness.
- Motion: none required; the material and typography carry the idea.
- Render: a premium editorial opening with the common copy, a restrained illustration and room for reading; no simulated product screenshots.
- Risk: may feel like a publication rather than a service; the eventual enquiry must be close to the argument and easy to find.

## C — The shared horizon

- Signature: the human and stone giant seen together from behind at a broad observatory window, studying the same golden landscape.
- World: dark bronze interior, evening gold outside, warm white typography, a single cinematic image.
- Surfaces: the story begins at the operator's eye level, then gives way to quiet readable evidence; the giant never dominates the person.
- Motion: optional subtle light shift, never scroll capture; static in reduced motion.
- Render: the common copy as confident editorial art direction over a calm part of the image, one perfect article-opening screen.
- Risk: cinematic scale can resemble another homepage; the article identity must remain clear.
