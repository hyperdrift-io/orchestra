# Orchestra AI — Mission

> **Agent orchestration for production AI workflows.**

## What we exist to do

Make agents production-grade inside the vertical SaaS products that already serve real customers. Close the gap between the AI demo and the feature your users can rely on — multi-tenant, audit-ready, cost-controlled, framework- and model-neutral.

We are *not* another agent framework. We are the engineering practice that takes the frameworks the industry already builds (LangGraph, CrewAI, Mastra, Claude / OpenAI Agent SDKs, MCP) and integrates them safely into systems that cannot afford to be wrong.

## Who this is for

**Primary ICP:** B2B vertical SaaS companies — Seed–Series B or bootstrapped past ~$1M ARR — whose product serves one specific traditional industry (construction, dental, legal ops, logistics, field service, real estate ops, healthcare ops, manufacturing, accounting, insurance brokerage, …).

**The buyer:** technical founder or CTO. They speak our language; we speak theirs.

**Their pain:** the product works. Their customers and competitors are pushing them toward AI features now. They cannot afford to pause the roadmap to retrain the team. They cannot afford to ship a feature that breaks multi-tenancy, audit, or cost discipline.

## What we believe

1. **Production is the only metric.** Agents that survive multi-tenancy, cost spikes, tool failures and live customer load are the only ones worth shipping.
2. **Specialisation compounds. Generalism dilutes.** Every engagement is a vertical SaaS or a production AI workflow. Every case study deepens the playbook for the next one.
3. **Model- and framework-neutral by stance.** OpenAI, Anthropic, local. LangGraph, CrewAI, Mastra. Your stack, your call — Orchestra adapts. We never lock you in.
4. **OSS as methodology, not marketing.** Patterns we apply repeatedly become opinionated libraries (multi-tenant agent isolation, MCP-from-schema, audit-grade logging). The methodology is inspectable.
5. **Pricing is per engagement.** Every product is different. We refuse the lie of a public price tag on bespoke work.
6. **No theatre.** No decks before scope. No anti-positioning. No 14-day stunts. Senior engineering, plainly stated.

## What success looks like

- A small portfolio of vertical SaaS clients who shipped AI features that survived production load.
- Two or three OSS libraries that fill real holes in the agent ecosystem — adopted because they solve problems nobody else does.
- A reputation that travels: when a vertical SaaS CTO needs an AI feature in production, our name comes up unprompted.
- Eventually, graduation from the Hyperdrift sub-brand to a standalone identity at the moment our own track record outweighs the studio's lineage.

## What we will not do

- Build yet-another general-purpose agent framework. The ecosystem is already crowded; our work is integration, not invention of new frameworks.
- Publish public pricing on bespoke service work.
- Position by attacking competitors. Differentiation comes from depth.
- Take engagements outside our ICP because they have budget. Discipline protects the brand.
- Ship OSS in a vacuum — every library is extracted from a real engagement.

## The studio

Orchestra AI is an offspring of [Hyperdrift](https://hyperdrift.io) — a studio of vertical SaaS products (Intel, HyperCV, Revela, Web3 Capital) built and run by a founding engineer with 25+ years of experience on advanced microservice AWS stacks, multi-billion-AUM platforms, and back-office surfaces that hold up under real production load.

We do not just integrate AI for vertical SaaS. We *run* vertical SaaS. That is the credibility we extend to every client engagement.
