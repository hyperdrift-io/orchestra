## The idea

Let a founder watch a business opportunity become work they can inspect. The distinctive asset is the living mesh: attention flows into a decision, becomes an improvement, then returns as learning. Every piece points to the same business question: what would be worth improving in the business you already have?

Use the approved proposition, **“Grow your business. Keep more of the upside.”** The audience has customers, active users, repeat work or credible demand. They want more commercial progress from the business they have built. AI engineering is the service; the mesh demonstrates the operating pattern. The homepage is an illustrative scenario, never a customer case study or a revenue forecast.

The campaign aims for qualified visibility: a relevant founder recognises an opportunity, inspects the work, then starts a useful conversation. Impressions alone cannot establish this.

## Why this should earn attention

The mesh gives the series a recognisable visual signature. A repeatable business scenario makes the unfamiliar service easier to understand. Inspectable demonstrations reduce uncertainty before asking for contact. Each article gives one useful answer without requiring a form; a relevant next step follows the answer. The partnership is self-selection for founders with traction, not scarcity theatre.

These are behavioural hypotheses, not promised conversion lifts. Use progressive detail, concrete evidence and low-friction next steps from the marketing-psychology skill; avoid unsupported “seven touches”, fake social proof or loss-aversion pressure. The business goal remains a qualified conversation.
