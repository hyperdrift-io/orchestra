# AI-native articles on ai.hyperdrift.io

Approved by the founder in conversation, 21 September 2026.

## Decision

New articles explaining the AI-native business proposition belong at `ai.hyperdrift.io/articles`. Every article should encourage a relevant sales enquiry and use a strong example Hyperdrift built when one exists. Existing published articles on `hyperdrift.io` retain their URLs initially and link into the new series where useful.

The commercial story includes AI inside a client's product and AI in how a client's business operates. The Bridge is the opening worked example. The anniversary provides the introduction into this series.

## Language

- Hyperdrift: organisation and umbrella brand.
- The fleet: the collection of products being operated.
- Hyperdrift Bridge on first mention, the Bridge thereafter: the experience through which an operator understands evidence and directs work.
- First Officer: the conversational role in the Bridge; current prototype status must remain explicit.
- Skills: written specialist guidance agents use for a job; never described as enforcement of system permissions.
- The Crew: a separately named service; not an interchangeable name for the Bridge.
- Orchestra: retain the existing public name unless a separate brand decision changes it.
