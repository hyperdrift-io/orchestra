# The page — three things a visitor relates to

17 September 2026. Founder direction: visitors relate to **proven expertise** (the graph), **actual deliveries** (case studies), and **their own problem** (how it solves it). The emotional core, in the founder's words: anyone can vibe-code something; what a founder needs is to keep every hour on the business and its growth, because everything else is a distraction that leads to failure. That line is for founders first, but a head of operations and a consultancy partner feel the same pull for their teams, so the page says it once, in a way all three recognise.

Voice Covenant on every line. No prices, terms or legal on the page (founder rule, 16 Sep). Graph spec: `GRAPH.md`.

## Structure

### 1. First screen: the graph, and what we do

The loop draws itself in: founder on top, Sense → Read → Work → Ship → Learn, the fifteen jobs lighting on their cadence. One line under the masthead says what we do. Draft directions (founder picks one, or rewrites):

- "We put a company on agents. You keep your eyes on the business."
- "Your company in full motion. You grow it; the agents run the rest."
- "Everything that isn't growth, handled. This is what it looks like."

Primary: "Describe your workflow". Quiet: "See the work". Nothing else on the first screen.

### 2. Proven expertise: the day, in movements

The graph's seven movements (`GRAPH.md`): 06:00 the run starts → Sense → Read → Work → Ship → Learn → one email. Each node opens its panel with the artefact. This is the "we run on this" proof and the architecture in miniature; the flagship article carries the reasoning.

### 3. Actual deliveries: case studies

Newest first, each with the company it was built for or with, "Built for" credit for contests, industry tag where a logo cannot be used, a live link where one exists:

1. Data and AI platform engineering for Vodafone Three, through Tecknuovo (current, telecom).
2. Deputy workforce MCP: five workforce workflows as agent tools, remote, published (workforce).
3. Next Role: a product exposed as an MCP server in the official registry (careers).
4. Standup, built for Amazon Web Services' Agents for Humans (submitted 14 Sep).
5. unanswered, built for DEV's Weekend Generosity, Google AI category (submitted).
6. uk.gov Radar, built for Devpost's WebMCP challenge with OpenAI (submitted).
7. Helm, built for Google's All Things Agentic (submitted).
8. The fleet: eighteen public apps run by one operator on the loop above.

Contest scouting (the agent that reads organiser mail, ledgers events, walks the phases with a hard founder gate before submission) is told here, as the story behind entries 4–7.

### 4. Your problem: where you plug in

The graph relabelled with the visitor's functions, by a radio toggle, no JavaScript:

- **A product team** putting agents into what it ships: the Ship and Work columns become their release and integration path; the MCP servers are the pattern.
- **An operations team** putting agents into how it runs: Sense, Learn and the founder's email become their monitoring, reconciliation and morning brief.
- **A consultancy** that owns the client and needs the build: the whole loop as delivery capacity, the Vodafone Three line as the reference.

Then the founder paragraph, once: the hours a company spends on everything that is not growth, and what it looks like when those hours come back. Ends on the door.

### 5. The door

"Describe one workflow." The existing enquiry form, reframed; records which relabel the visitor chose. Promise: a written read within one working day on whether it is a fit and what the first step would be. Supporting lines already in use: "You keep the code", "No decks, no discovery theatre." The Traction Partnership sits beside it as an open door, no terms.

### 6. After the door

Latest writing and Intel (live feeds), the ship log if it lands in this cut, footer. Long material folded.

## Not on the page

Prices, partnership terms, legal. Numbers as proof. Fear framing. Client logos without written permission. Anything implying an organiser's endorsement.
