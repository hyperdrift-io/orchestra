# Growth rings

The founder feels that useful progress is retained: the next decision starts from a stronger business, with more capacity to grow revenue, protect margin and lead.

## Signature

A sculptural core of translucent annual rings and nested luminous shells. Useful learning becomes part of the material, rather than disappearing when an animation loops.

## The world

Near-black ink, warm ivory Cormorant serif, amber light inside polished dark glass. Five smaller spherical domains carry restrained jade, blue, violet, coral and gold accents. This is institutional intelligence made tangible, not a technical network diagram.

## Every surface

The dedicated organisation exploration is one continuous specimen. Selecting a domain enters its material and reveals the relevant business action. Returning to the core reveals the retained learning across the whole business. The opening screen remains spacious and quiet around one dominant sculptural form.

## Motion

Signals branch along fine filaments, trigger local responses and converge into the core. A validated improvement settles into a new luminous ring; unsuccessful work fades without increasing the core. Existing rings remain visible as a memory of previous useful iterations. Reduced motion presents the settled core and permits direct domain selection.

## Render brief

One landscape desktop screen, approximately 1536 by 1024. A remarkable sculptural growth core fills the lower two thirds of an ink-black field: translucent nested amber shells and fine annual rings, with five smaller differently coloured glass spheres connected by branching gold signal filaments. Fine light packets visibly arrive and divide at intersections. Retained inner rings remain calm while the outermost ring is being formed. Premium physically rendered material, generous negative space, refined contrast. Only two lines of warm ivory typography: “Every useful signal can become progress.” and “Learn. Improve. Build on what works.” No extra labels, metrics, controls or page skeleton.

## Risk

The metaphor can become beautiful but abstract. Domain interaction must reveal a concrete scenario immediately, and ring formation must represent measured learning rather than imply guaranteed profit after every cycle. The concept is an illustrative direction, not evidence of client results.
