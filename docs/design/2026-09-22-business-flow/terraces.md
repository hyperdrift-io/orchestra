# Direction C — Terraces of progress

Mission: the founder feels their business can build on what it has already learned, with visible room to grow revenue, protect margin and reclaim time.

## Signature

A luminous landscape grows in terraces. Useful signals branch into streams, gather in five coloured spherical reservoirs and spread across a newly illuminated contour. The accumulated terraces make retained progress visible without a fictitious growth chart.

## The world

Near-black ink, obsidian contour terrain and warm ivory Cormorant-like serif typography. Hairline gold contours give structure; jade, blue, violet, coral and gold spherical reservoirs carry each domain's character. Light appears valuable because most of the world stays quiet.

## Every surface

The dedicated exploration page opens onto one uninterrupted landscape. Domains are places to enter, rather than boxes to read. Selecting a reservoir reveals the signal, action, impact and measurement within that part of the business. The current scenario and the founder's next move can remain live accessible text outside the artwork in the eventual implementation.

## Motion

Signals divide along real tributaries, pause in a reservoir as a decision forms, then activate several downstream routes. A validated improvement lights a new terrace while previous successful work remains visible. An unsuccessful experiment recedes without erasing the learning. Reduced motion shows a stable illuminated iteration with explicit step controls. This is illustrative accumulation, never a promise that every iteration creates growth.

## Image brief

One high-fidelity landscape desktop screen, approximately 1536 × 1024. An immersive oblique aerial view of exquisitely detailed luminous terraced terrain, branching gold signal streams and five small translucent coloured spherical reservoirs. Lower established terraces glow softly; the newest outer contour brightens to show progress retained. Generous dark sky at the top holds only the two lines below. No navigation, form, card grid or diagram skeleton.

“Every useful signal can become progress.”

“Learn. Improve. Build on what works.”

## Risk

The landscape is emotionally powerful but less immediately literal than a network. The eventual interaction must tie each stream to a real business scenario and state clearly that the illuminated terrain represents learning and useful work, not measured revenue.
