## Visual and interaction decisions

The shared family is vertex-and-edge geometry only. Notice is an eye, Choose a signpost, Improve a wrench, Release a folded paper plane and Learn an open book. Each internal actor is also literal: conversation bubbles, a magnifier, lightbulb, chart, checked decision, target, scales, flag, hammer, open door, clock, checklist, shield, parcel, response pulse, comparison chart, books, steps and coins. Assets share line weight, vertex treatment, restrained rotation and domain colour. There are no downloaded models, filled materials, texture maps or generated raster domain assets.

The homepage uses a uniformly scaled spherical network inside a larger sphere that reaches the viewport edges. Domain anchors use the same rotation as the network. Hover brings a domain forward; click or tap enters it. Native labels remain keyboard operable. Fixed domain links sit beside the sphere on desktop and above it on phones, providing stable entrances even when geometry overlaps the business core. Escape restores focus to that domain’s fixed link. Camera position, target, distance and field of view use a continuous spring with retained velocity. Click-out and Escape return. Actor geometry and its label both open its business detail. The canvas keeps its dimensions throughout entry and return. A small pause/resume button remains inside the scene; the control row and numbered navigation below it are removed.

Each domain returns a coloured contribution along a connected curve to the central business sphere. Arrival produces a brief, low-amplitude increase in mesh brightness. The core and inner network retain gradual, uniform growth, capped after fifteen contributions; later cycles continue without implying unlimited improvement. The camera does not respond to the heartbeat or reinforcement. This is an explicitly illustrative business scenario, not measured customer growth or a revenue projection.

## Commercial journey

The founder’s latest request moves the illustrative scenario and animation to the opening screen. A concrete customer-growth headline gives the animation a purpose; the full growth/profit proposition follows immediately below. The contact link remains visible in the masthead.

The homepage addresses founders with customers, active users or credible demand and a commitment to the next stage. The prominent Traction Partnership invitation follows the three inspectable builds and links to its full terms and a contextual enquiry. Copy enables founders and builds on their existing strengths; it does not use shame, scarcity claims or guaranteed growth. These choices apply the marketing-psychology and CRO review through relevance, inspectable proof, clear qualification and a low-friction next step. No conversion improvement is claimed without data.

The enquiry and proof heading issue came from limiting the border box in characters while applying viewport-sized horizontal padding inside it. Horizontal spacing now uses margins, preserving a usable text width at desktop and phone sizes.
