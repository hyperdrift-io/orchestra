## Intent

The founder feels that their business has a clear route forward and capable people alongside them. Two solid forms create an ascending passage in the space between them: coordination becomes progress. The mark is a compact architectural seal, with the route discovered through negative space.

Signature: the single ascending void. The world is flat ink, muted gold, ivory and editorial typography. The same silhouette works in a masthead, favicon, document seal and small monochrome signature. The static form carries the identity without needing motion.

Strength: progress and coordination are present in one compact shape, with strong monochrome potential. Trade-off: its abstract architectural quality communicates orchestration indirectly; reduction must preserve the central channel.
