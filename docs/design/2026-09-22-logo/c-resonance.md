# C — Resonance

Status: concept for founder selection; no adoption or production asset change.

## Rationale

The founder should feel that the business they have already built is the steady centre, with useful expertise and action gathering around it.

The signature is a calm central oval revealed by three coordinated, broad curved forms. The positive shapes carry controlled movement; their shared negative space holds the business core. Unequal curve lengths and a slight upward bias provide rhythm without an arrow. The shape should read as one compact emblem at small sizes.

The world is flat muted gold on warm ink, paired with the existing ivory editorial serif wordmark. The mark needs no material effect, framing device or animation. If animated later, a single gentle arrival into alignment would express coordination; reduced motion would use the settled mark.

Primary strength: a compact, ownable symbol for different contributions becoming one coordinated business.

Trade-off: the meaning is more abstract than a literal monogram, and final vector refinement must preserve the open counter at favicon size.

The existing ink, cream and gold direction is approved. This logo is a new mark and awaits explicit founder selection. All user-facing copy follows `meta/PHILOSOPHY.md` §8, Speak to Enable.
