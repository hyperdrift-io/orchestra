# A — Aperture

Status: concept for founder selection. Existing ink, ivory and gold identity is retained; this new mark is not approved for implementation.

## Concept

The founder feels clarity and momentum: their customer signals become a useful next move. A single, compact O encloses an A revealed by negative space. The enclosing form suggests coordinated work; the opening suggests a clear direction. It is a typographic monogram, never a literal diagram of an AI system.

The signature is the second reading: first a calm circular emblem, then the A inside it. Warm gold against near-black gives the mark the same editorial restraint as the existing site. The serif wordmark remains the human voice; the symbol is reduced and architectural. It needs no motion to explain itself, and any future reveal would preserve the complete static silhouette for reduced motion.

Strength: immediate silhouette and a compact initial-based identity suited to a favicon, social avatar and small navigation mark.

Trade-off: circular letter monograms are familiar territory. Distinctiveness depends on the exact countershape, optical proportions and generous negative-space opening rather than decorative details. Vector construction and a small-size optical pass would follow selection.
