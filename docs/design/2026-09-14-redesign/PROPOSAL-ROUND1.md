# Round 1 — Identity, open exploration

[Brief](BRIEF.md) · previous packet: [stage-entry round 1](../2026-09-14-stage-entry/PROPOSAL-ROUND1.md) (board B, implemented and tagged `stage-entry-b-2026-09-14`).

**Operator brief.** Founder retired the Concert Hall × Lab Notebook identity on 14 September and asked for a brand-new redesign, scope confirmed as open exploration. The approved content and interaction (stage entry, folded long material, dated feeds, visible partnership) are locked; only the identity varies. Three directions, each a different relationship with the visitor: A collaborator at the shared desk, B guide on a route map, C co-pilot in a control room at dusk. Nine trials, seeds 101–109, `recraftv4_1_utility_pro`, 2:3.

**Recommendation: direction B, the route map.** Take B-105's station entry (map, numbered stations, marker on 02, panel beside the map, vertical route on mobile) as the interaction and B-104's ground, palette and page structure as the shell. It is the only direction whose identity *is* the founder's question, "where are you", rather than decoration around it. C is the usable fallback if the founder wants to stay dark; A did not find its identity in this round.
