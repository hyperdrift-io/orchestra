## The site as one walk — recommended sequence

1. **First screen: 5C, the walk and its footprints.** Seen from behind, the founder on the giant's shoulder points at the waking city, and every footprint the giant left behind has grown a village. Understanding, direction, prosperity and proof in one frame. Flaw to fix: the giant is cut at the waist and reads as a torso standing in the field; it needs its legs mid-stride.
2. **The door: 5D, four places on the horizon.** The giant's head at the edge of the frame, the founder on its shoulder pointing, and the four situations as four lit places in one landscape: a light on a hill, water channels into fields, a bridge, the city. The approved stage entry becomes a moment in the world instead of a list. Flaws: the founder points at the first place, and the labels float above the places rather than sitting on them.
3. **The proof: 5E, where the giant has walked, things grow.** Aerial trail of footprints holding an orchard, a harbour, a workshop. The line works as written. Flaw: the founder, in a red coat, stands on the giant's head, not its shoulder; the shoulder is the promise.
4. **The enquiry: 5F, "You say where. We go."** The giant lowers its face towards the founder's pointing hand, calm and attentive. The strongest expression of "understands your business" in five rounds. Flaw: the arm's scale is ambiguous and can read as the giant's own.

## The others

- **5H-a, portrait page.** The giant walks and points, the founder stands on its outstretched arm pointing the same way. Two figures, one direction: a beautiful single poster and the best candidate for the mobile first screen. Founder on the arm rather than the shoulder.
- **5B, over the shoulder.** The quietest image: the founder on a mossy shoulder beside the giant's stone face, looking out. Calm and safe; nobody points, and the giant reads as a cliff.
- **5A, refined.** Close to the picked 4A; kept as the fallback first screen, no new idea.
- **5G-a** (the giant's head as a mountain) loses the walk. **5G-b** puts a real London skyline behind a small giant and loses the scale. **5H-b** bows the giant's head with the founder standing on it, which reads as weight rather than lift. Pass on all three.
