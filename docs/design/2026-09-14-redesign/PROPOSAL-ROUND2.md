# Round 2 — Soul, under the growth register

[Round-2 brief](BRIEF-ROUND2.md) · parent: [Round 1](PROPOSAL-ROUND1.md)

**Operator brief (founder).** "Too generic. No soul. We're here to fast track growth via AI integration. It's a clear path that leads to profit. We're also disrupting the old ways and moving to 10x productivity with a proven track record." Axis under test: identity intensity under that register. One message layer changed from round 1 (hero statement, proof strip, old way / new way); everything else locked. Three new directions: D the fast track, E the receipts, F the manifesto. Nine trials, seeds 201–209; two per direction on `recraftv4_1_utility_pro`, one on `recraftv4_1_pro`.

**Recommendation: E-204, the receipts.** It answers the founder's brief most directly: the proven track record is the first thing on the page, shown as a shipping ledger with organiser credit and honest stamps, and the four situations are physical work orders. Two transplants make it complete: F-208's crossed-out old way for the old/new block, and D-203's orange track heat for the rail that already runs across E-204's top. Both are named elements with a place, not a merge of everything.

Voice check across the round: no board invented a metric, a logo, an award or a testimonial. Invented content was garble and duplication (extra numerals, fabricated organiser pairings in E-205/E-206 and D-201's livery). All of it is layout-approved, value-forbidden; the real ledger rows come from `src/data/case-studies.ts`.

- `recraftv4_1_pro` delivers the atmosphere (D-203, F-209, E-206) and breaks the skeleton every time; `utility_pro` keeps the page. Use pro for the reference mood, utility for the board to map.

- The founder's register survived the Voice Covenant without hype: "fast track to growth" as a statement, "proven" as a ledger, "disrupting" as a strike. No numbers were needed.
