# Round 3 — concept candidates, submitted for founder review

[Concept in words](CONCEPT.md) · parent: [Round 2](PROPOSAL-ROUND2.md). Founder rule for this round: the concept is generated from images; we are creating an idea, not code; ScreenCraft is a tool for implementing the concept, nothing more. Nothing here is a page layout and nothing is restricted by what CSS can do.

![Contact sheet](round3-concept/contact-sheet.png)

Per world: the world itself (16:9, the thing Recraft photographs), then two holistic concept images of the whole site as an idea (2:3), all on `recraftv4_1_pro`, seeds 301–332, sidecars beside each PNG. Copy in the images is two sentences only; garbled letters are the model, not the idea.

## 01 The Engine, running
`01-the-engine.png` · `01-concept-a.png` · `01-concept-b.png`
The site is one small precise engine on a test stand, running while the new part goes in. Concept b sets the statement at poster scale over the lit stand with the tagged parts wall (the proof) behind and the shift log under the lamp; concept a is quieter, with the four stations as parts along the shaft. Read: the strongest "AI inside your product while it keeps earning". Risk: reads industrial before it reads software.

## 02 The Line
`02-the-line.png` · `02-concept-a.png` · `02-concept-b.png`
The site is one plotted ink line on paper, the growth curve and the path to profit at once; the statement follows the line, the ticks are the four situations, the plotter's log along the bottom is the proof. Concept b (charcoal line, type on the curve, vermilion wet tip) is the most original image of three rounds. Read: the founder's sentence made literal, quiet enough for a CTO. Risk: austerity; the ink has to stay real.

## 03 The Split
`03-the-split.png` · `03-concept-a.png` · `03-concept-b.png`
Every screen is one room cut down the middle: the old way at night on the left, the bench at dawn on the right; "the old way" struck through; the question stands in the light. Concept b's hero is the stronger cut. Read: the loudest "disrupting the old way". Risk: the repeated thumbnails below the hero show how quickly the device becomes a trope; the idea lives in the first screen only.

## What the founder is asked

Pick a world to push further in images, ask for a different world, or combine (for example the Line's path with the Engine's proof wall). No boards, no code, no ScreenCraft until the founder says the concept is the one to build.
