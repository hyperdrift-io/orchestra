# Round 4 — the giant: concept candidates

Founder brief: "User should feel they will stand on the shoulder of a giant that will understand their business and drive it to prosperity." Concept worlds in [CONCEPT.md → Round 4](CONCEPT.md). Six images, `recraftv4_1_pro`, seeds 401–406, one wide first screen and one tall page concept per world. Two sentences of copy. No chrome, no code.

![Contact sheet](round4-giant/contact-sheet.png)

- **4A On the shoulder** (`4A-screen.png`, `4A-page.png`): the giant made visible, weathered stone, walking through golden country toward a waking city; the founder on its shoulder points, and the giant looks where they point. The wide screen is the strongest single image of four rounds: understanding is literal, prosperity is the horizon, the founder is tiny and the sharpest thing in the frame.
- **4B The view** (`4B-screen.png`, `4B-page.png`): the giant implied by altitude; the founder on a shoulder-shaped rock above the cloud, their town and fields below. Beautiful, but without the giant it reads as a landscape; the feeling of being carried is missing.
- **4C The lift** (`4C-screen.png`, `4C-page.png`): colossal careful hands raising a small lit workshop above ripe wheat. Understanding as care lands; the house-in-hands tips toward greeting card, the wide crop less so.

Recommendation: **4A**, wide screen as the first screen of the site. Push it further in images before anything else: the four situations as four places the founder can point to on the horizon (a first step, a workflow, a prototype, a live product), the proof as what the giant has already carried, the enquiry as the founder's pointing hand.
