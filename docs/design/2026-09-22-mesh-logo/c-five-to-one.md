Concept only. Built-in ImageGen; no production assets or UI changed.

## Signature

A sparse gold spherical mesh converges toward a negative-space business core. Five contributing domains form one coherent business, with the empty center hinting at an O and retaining a connection to Aperture.

## World

Founders already have momentum; coordinated contributions help turn it into growth and profit. The image conveys collected effort and calm direction, without promising unsupported outcomes. Voice covenant: enable, never diminish; strengths first, evidence second, next move third.

## Surfaces

## Motion

Five colored domain signals briefly traverse selected gold edges inward; the central contour strengthens subtly when a contribution arrives. Keep scale fixed. Reduced motion uses the static mark.

## Risk

The generated center resembles a keyhole, and the precise five-sector count is not yet explicit. In an approved vector rendition, close the core opening and make the five contributions geometrically exact. At very small sizes, simplify edges further. This is a direction study rather than a final master.

## Final prompt

Center a refined gold wireframe sphere icon in the upper two thirds, about 450 pixels diameter. Use roughly 25 thin straight gold line segments and 15 tiny gold vertex dots arranged as five triangular wedge sectors meeting around one small circular empty center. The silhouette must be circular like a sphere. Five mesh sectors communicate five domains contributing to one core. Sparse, crisp, vector style. Uniform muted gold #dca958. No glow, gradients, textures, grain, filled polygons, or lighting effects. Below: exact centered text Orchestra AI in elegant ivory serif, and small tracked BY HYPERDRIFT. Generous negative space, premium minimal identity presentation. No decorative circular orbits; a triangulated spherical mesh minimal enough to adapt to a 32px favicon.
