# A — Living Core

Concept exploration only. No production asset or UI changed. Generated with built-in image_gen; strongest result retained following an opacity/line-cleanliness refinement.

## Signature
A sparse triangular spherical network, with one luminous business core. Every line appears to contribute to the same centre, directly connecting the identity with the homepage's business mesh.

## World
Calm confidence: a founder's existing momentum becomes a coordinated, growing business. Gold light against ink; generous negative space; editorial serif typography.

## Surfaces
Header mark: simplify rear edges and use approximately 12–24 vertices at 44px. Favicon: stronger boundary and a much reduced set of core connections at 16/32px. Open Graph: full network and restrained core light. Retain square proportions at every size.

## Motion
The same slow shared rotation as the homepage mesh. Selected signals can travel inward, briefly reinforcing the core. No geometric scaling/heartbeat zoom. Reduced motion is a static mark.

## Risk
Most directly related to the homepage, but less proprietary than a monogram and still too detailed for a literal favicon reduction. An approved vector master should simplify topology at small sizes, remove rear-edge clutter, and preserve spherical depth. This concept is not a finished production vector.

Generated native image is square (1254px), despite requested 1024px; no aspect ratio distortion. A refinement removed the initial unwanted alpha background/fringing. Fine lines and subtle glow are illustrative, subject to deliberate SVG translation after direction approval.
