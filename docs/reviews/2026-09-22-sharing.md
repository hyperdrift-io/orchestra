# Sharing readiness — 22 September 2026

Requested: keep Open Graph current and make every AI-site article easy to share. This is a compatible addition to the approved article design and Mesh Aperture identity.

## Delivered

- One reusable `ArticleShare` component on every article, reached by a “Share article” shortcut beside the byline and placed alongside the article takeaway.
- Copy link, LinkedIn, email, native sharing where available, and download-card controls. Links use the clean canonical HTTPS article URL, never the preview host, query parameters or reader-provided data.
- Copy failure reveals a selectable field rather than reporting success. Native cancellation remains quiet; other failures point to the available alternatives. No success claim or conversion is recorded for opening a composer or share sheet.
- Progressive enhancement: LinkedIn, email and image download are ordinary links. Browser-only controls appear after hydration. Controls have at least 44px height, visible keyboard focus, wrapped mobile layout and an accessible status message.
- The draft label explicitly explains that public links work after publication. Draft visibility and publication dates remain unchanged.
- Default and article Open Graph/Twitter images include descriptions, dimensions and PNG MIME type. Article metadata carries site name, locale, author and an actual publication date when present. Website metadata retains consistent defaults across child routes; homepage canonical is explicit.
- Share-image URLs carry a digest of logo geometry and displayed copy. Article structured data and downloads reference the same versioned image. Article image responses are revalidated daily. Platform page caches may still require a re-scrape after deployment; changing an image URL does not force a platform to revisit a page.

## Verification

Typecheck, isolated production build and whitespace checks passed. Read-only local checks covered all eight article routes: canonical URL, article type, image alt text, image dimensions, matching OG/Twitter URL and share controls. All eight images returned PNGs at 1200 × 630, approximately 46–55 KB. Five website routes also passed canonical, locale, website type, dimensions and large-image Twitter metadata checks. The newly generated article images preserve the approved logo.

Desktop and 390px phone browser review confirmed the sharing row wraps without horizontal overflow, every control is at least 44px tall and the byline shortcut reaches the sharing section. Clicking Copy link reached its success status. The automation clipboard read returned empty, so clipboard contents could not be independently verified. The native handler entered its pending state; the host's system share sheet could not be inspected through browser automation. No LinkedIn post, email or native share was sent. The preview tab was reloaded afterwards.

References: [Web Share API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/share), [Clipboard writeText](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Clipboard/writeText), and the existing legacy Hyperdrift share block. The AI site reuses the same canonical-link and reader-driven sharing approach without importing the legacy app's dependencies or analytics contracts.
